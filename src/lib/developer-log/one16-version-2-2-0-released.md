We released version 2.2.0 to the App Store. This update has been a long time coming: One16 has moved from paid upfront to freemium.

The decision was not easy. Until Apple allows app trials, it is difficult to help customers understand how One16 fits their workflow before spending money. The new model lets people download the app, explore the features, and upgrade to One16 Pro when it makes sense.

## Current customers

People who bought One16 before 2.2.0 receive all current and future Pro features without another purchase. We implemented a receipt-based path to unlock those features, because honoring that original trust matters.

We are grateful to everyone who supported One16 early on, and we are excited to put it in the hands of more woodworkers.
